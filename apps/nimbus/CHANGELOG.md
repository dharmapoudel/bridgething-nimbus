# Nimbus

## 1.0.2

- Initial store release.
- Now, Week, Details and Places screens: big serif temperature, next hours,
  8-day forecast with a temperature curve, and saved places with their own
  sky, local time and temperature.
- Animated sky backdrops that follow the weather and time of day, with rain,
  snow, stars, mist and lightning on a single canvas.
- Forecasts from Open-Meteo (free, no API key), current location from the
  phone's GPS, saved cities from the companion settings page.
- Knob scrubs through the next 24 hours on Now; top button refreshes.
